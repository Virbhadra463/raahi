Local & Experiences — Intelligent Local Discovery & Experience Platform

Problem Statement:

When travelers visit a new destination, they often want to experience more than popular tourist attractions. They may be interested in local food, cultural experiences, festivals, workshops, adventure activities, hidden places, local events, shopping, nightlife, or experiences hosted by local communities. However, discovering these experiences is often difficult because information is spread across different websites, social media platforms, booking portals, review platforms, and local listings.

The challenge becomes more complex because travelers have different interests, budgets, schedules, group sizes, accessibility requirements, and travel preferences. A traveler may have only three hours available, a family may be looking for child-friendly activities, or a visitor may want experiences within a particular budget and distance from their hotel. Generic search and recommendation platforms often do not consider all these factors together.

At the same time, local restaurants, tour operators, activity providers, artists, event organizers, guides, and small businesses may struggle to reach travelers who are genuinely interested in their offerings. Many local experiences remain difficult to discover even when they are highly relevant to visitors.
Hackathon Challenge
Design and prototype an intelligent local discovery and experience platform that connects travelers with relevant local experiences while helping local businesses and experience providers reach the right customers.

The platform should create a personalized discovery experience where travelers can search or receive recommendations based on who they are, where they are, what they like, how much time they have, how much they want to spend, and what they are already planning to do.

The solution should consider factors such as: Traveler interests and preferences, Current or planned location, Available time, Budget, Distance and travel time, Experience availability, Opening hours, Ratings and reviews, Group size and traveler type, Accessibility requirements and Existing itinerary

For example, instead of simply showing a list of restaurants or activities, the platform could understand that a family has two hours available near their hotel and wants an affordable local food and cultural experience, and recommend suitable options that can realistically fit within that time.

The system should also be capable of adapting recommendations when circumstances change. For example, if an activity becomes unavailable, weather conditions change, the traveler has less time than expected, or their budget changes, the platform could suggest suitable alternatives.   

Teams may use AI, recommendation systems, location intelligence, personalization, data analytics, or conversational interfaces to improve discovery and planning.

On the provider side, the platform may allow local businesses and experience providers to create listings, manage availability, define offerings, understand traveler preferences, and promote relevant experiences.

The goal is to build a solution that moves beyond traditional search-and-list platforms and creates a more intelligent connection between travelers looking for meaningful local experiences and local businesses looking for relevant customers.

Personalized Dynamic Tour Planning & Tour Operations Platform
Problem Statement
Traditional tour operators typically offer pre-designed packages with fixed destinations, hotels, transportation, activities, schedules, and pricing. While such packages simplify tour management, they provide limited flexibility for travelers who have different interests, budgets, travel durations, accommodation preferences, activity choices, and preferred travel styles. Modern travelers increasingly expect to customize their journeys rather than simply select from predefined packages.

At the same time, delivering personalized tours creates significant operational challenges for tour operators. They need to coordinate customers, hotels, transportation providers, activity vendors, tour coordinators, payments, schedules, group participants, and itinerary changes. When a traveler modifies a requirement or an unexpected situation occurs during the journey, operators may need to manually coordinate multiple stakeholders and update several parts of the itinerary, making the process inefficient and difficult to manage.
Hackathon Challenge
Design and prototype a personalized and dynamic tour planning and operations platform that connects travelers and tour operators through a single system and supports the complete lifecycle of a customized tour. The platform should allow travelers to define their destination preferences, travel dates, duration, budget, accommodation preferences, transportation choices, interests, activities, and preferred travel style, and then create and customize their own tour rather than being restricted to a fixed package. Travelers should be able to explore destinations and experiences, select and modify different components of their trip, compare alternatives, view estimated costs, receive an optimized itinerary, complete bookings, and access their complete travel plan.

The platform should also provide tour operators with a centralized environment to manage the operational side of personalized tours, including customers, bookings, vendors, hotels, transportation, activities, payments, tour groups, coordinators, schedules, and itinerary changes. The system should provide visibility into both the customer-facing journey and the operator-facing operations, allowing operators to efficiently manage multiple customized tours and coordinate the different service providers involved.

A key aspect of the challenge is dynamic tour management. Travel plans can change before or during a tour because of traveler preferences, availability issues, cancellations, delays, weather, or other unexpected situations. The platform should be able to identify the impact of such changes and help travelers or operators modify the itinerary while considering factors such as cost, availability, timing, location, dependencies, and traveler preferences.

Teams may use AI, recommendation systems, optimization, conversational interfaces, or intelligent automation to provide personalized destination and activity recommendations, generate and optimize itineraries, manage budgets, detect conflicts, suggest alternatives, and provide assistance during the journey.

The complete journey can be represented as:
Discover → Personalize → Plan → Price → Book → Prepare → Operate → Assist → Adapt → Complete → Review

The challenge is to demonstrate how technology can help tour operators move beyond fixed-package tourism toward a more personalized, flexible, and dynamically managed travel experience, while giving operators the operational visibility and control required to manage the entire tour lifecycle.

Mega-Event Hospitality Orchestration — Intelligent Capacity & Crowd Management
Problem Statement
When a city hosts a mega-event such as a major conference, national sports tournament, large concert, festival, or religious gathering, thousands or even millions of visitors may arrive within a short period. This sudden demand can put significant pressure on hotels, short-term accommodation, transportation, restaurants, venues, and other local services.

These resources are often managed independently, making it difficult for event organizers, hospitality providers, and city authorities to understand the overall situation. Certain areas may become overcrowded while accommodation remains available elsewhere, transportation networks may experience severe congestion, and travelers may struggle to find suitable accommodation or efficient routes. Lack of coordinated information can result in capacity shortages, uneven crowd distribution, higher costs, operational bottlenecks, and a poor visitor experience.
Hackathon Challenge
Design and prototype an intelligent event-driven hospitality orchestration platform that helps coordinate accommodation, transportation, and visitor movement during periods of extreme or sudden demand.

The platform should bring together relevant information about accommodation availability, transportation capacity, event schedules, visitor demand, locations, and crowd movement to provide a consolidated view of the event ecosystem. It should help identify areas approaching capacity, predict potential congestion or resource shortages, and support better distribution of visitors across the destination.

The solution should consider scenarios such as hotel saturation, transportation congestion, sudden demand spikes, venue capacity limits, last-mile connectivity, uneven visitor distribution, and changes in event schedules. It should help event organizers and hospitality stakeholders understand where pressure is building and take appropriate actions before the situation becomes critical.

Teams may develop intelligent mechanisms to recommend alternative accommodation zones, optimize attendee movement, suggest alternative transportation routes, predict crowd and capacity patterns, recommend off-peak travel, or provide incentives to distribute demand across less congested areas. AI, predictive analytics, optimization, recommendation systems, or simulation techniques may be used to support these decisions.

The platform may provide different experiences for different stakeholders, such as a command and decision-support interface for event organizers and hospitality operators, along with a planning and guidance interface for attendees.

The challenge is to demonstrate how technology can transform fragmented event and hospitality operations into a coordinated, data-driven system that anticipates demand, balances available capacity, reduces localized bottlenecks, and improves the overall experience for visitors and service providers during major events.
